Joohyun Lee 이주현

I am an M.S. student in Chemical Engineering at Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Felix Sunjoo Kim in the Organic Nanomaterials Lab. I have worked in his laboratory since 2022, first as an undergraduate researcher.

My research sits at the interface between conjugated polymer chemistry and organic electrochemical devices: I synthesize organic mixed ionic–electronic conductors (OMIECs) and engineer the electrolytes that control how they are doped and how they switch.

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.
