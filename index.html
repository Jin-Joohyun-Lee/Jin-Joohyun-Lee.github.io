Joohyun Lee 이주현

I am an M.S. student in Chemical Engineering at Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Felix Sunjoo Kim. I have worked in his laboratory since 2022, first as an undergraduate researcher.

My research sits at the interface between conjugated polymer chemistry and organic electrochemical devices. I synthesize organic mixed ionic–electronic conductors (OMIECs) whose energy levels are set at the monomer stage, and I engineer the electrolyte of organic electrochemical transistors (OECTs) to control how far the channel is doped and where it switches.

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

Research

I am interested in how the chemical structure of a conjugated polymer, its counterion, and its surrounding electrolyte set the physical and electronic behavior of mixed conductors, and in using that link to design materials and devices for bioelectronics.

  • Acceptor-engineered EDOT monomers — tuning the energy levels of OMIECs through the central unit of D-A-D monomers, polymerized in water (PSS-templated) and directly on substrates (tosylate).
  • Electrolyte engineering of OECTs — DBU-based acid–base ionic liquids that de-dope PEDOT:PSS and shift the threshold voltage by up to 500 mV, enabling enhancement-mode operation and OECT inverters.
